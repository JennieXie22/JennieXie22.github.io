Jianing Xie 解佳宁

I am an undergraduate student in Qiuzhen College at Tsinghua University, majoring in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics.

My research interests lie in causal inference, reinforcement learning, and applied statistics, especially methods motivated by healthcare and other real-world decision problems. I am currently working on causal inference for assisted reproduction with Prof. Linbo Wang and on safe offline reinforcement learning with Prof. Ruoqing Zhu.

Research

I am interested in developing statistically principled methods for learning from observational and offline data. Current themes include identification under missingness and multi-stage treatment, overlap and unmeasured confounding, and risk-sensitive policy learning.

Causal Inference for Multi-stage Assisted Reproduction
With Prof. Linbo Wang and clinical collaborators
Research Project, 2026–Present

Studying causal effects in a multi-stage assisted-reproduction setting with treatment timing, incomplete follow-up, and intermediate outcomes. I work on data construction and analyses using the g-formula, inverse-probability weighting/censoring weighting, and separable-effect ideas.

Tail-Constrained Offline Reinforcement Learning
With Prof. Ruoqing Zhu
Research Project, 2026–Present

Developing offline policy-learning methods that control rare but severe cumulative-cost events. The project explores density-ratio estimation, actor–critic optimization, Fitted Q-Iteration, and conservative evaluation under distribution shift.

Inverse Problems for Partial Differential Equations
Undergraduate Research Project, 2025

Investigated a medically motivated inverse problem governed by partial differential equations, with an emphasis on mathematical modeling, numerical reconstruction, and scientific computing.
